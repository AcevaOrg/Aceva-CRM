## Known limits and open decisions

1. **Who counts as sales staff (corrected).** Active employees of the Sales department (`dep-sales`) who hold `sales.own`,
   and the sales managers (holders of `sales.targets`). The default Level 4 role is `sales`, so the role alone is not
   used: Level 4 HR, Finance or Engineering employees are excluded. The same rule applies to targets, logs, reports, the
   dashboard and the notification recipients (sales managers). Reports already built are never changed.
2. Employees on approved leave still get a report for a working day (shown as not met when targets are set).
3. In live mode the Commission tab of the targets screen still shows demo data (M15.7 is not built).
